Description

Società Agricola Bergamin is an agricultural company and butcher shop that brings together farming tradition, quality meat and a modern customer experience. Alongside its physical shop, the brand offers an online store, a loyalty card, seasonal boxes and recurring promotions, speaking to its customers with a warm, direct and recognisable tone.

My role in the project

I managed Bergamin’s social media presence, with a strong focus on the visual side of every post. I designed the graphics for recurring series such as the Cut of the Month, for seasonal campaigns like Halloween and Christmas, and for the launch of new initiatives: the BergaCard loyalty card, the online shop, the WhatsApp channel, the BergAperitivo line and the AI-inspired character MacellAIo. I also produced short reels and video content for Instagram and Facebook. Beyond social media, I contributed to the creation of printed materials for the butcher shop’s promotions, such as posters, scratch cards and gift cards, keeping the same visual identity consistent between the digital and the physical world.
