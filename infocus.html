Description

InFocus is a mobile app designed to help students track and optimize their study time. The app allows users to track time spent on various tasks and activities through the use of a built-in timer, providing a clear and detailed overview of study habits. This tool is designed to improve time management and increase study efficiency.

My role in the project

In this university project, my colleagues and I worked on defining the design system, establishing the visual and functional guidelines of the app. We developed layouts and mock-ups to represent the user interface, and created prototypes to test and refine the navigation flow. Furthermore, we conducted a preliminary questionnaire to understand user needs and subsequently carried out user tests to collect feedback and improve the overall user experience.
