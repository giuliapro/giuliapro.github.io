Description

Eazy Group is a yachting group that supports captains, owners and yacht managers worldwide. Three business units cover yacht fuel and oil supply (Eazy Bunker), maritime agency and concierge services (Eazy Yachting) and ecological cleaning products for life on board (Eazy Green). The new website brings the three worlds together in one clear, multilingual experience, designed to help a busy captain find the right service and get in touch in a few seconds.

My role in the project

I took care of the whole graphic side of the website: art direction, the navy and sky-blue colour system, the oversized typographic headlines, the full-bleed yacht photography with dark overlays and the glassy cards used for services, testimonials and contact requests. I then built the site myself, from front-end to back-end: I prototyped and generated the React interface with Lovable, refined components with HTML, CSS and JavaScript, set up the multilingual pages (English, Italian and French), the quote and contact forms and the news section, and prepared the visuals and short videos that support the brand online.
