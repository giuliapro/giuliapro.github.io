Description

Valentino Tessuti is a home décor business based in Crema with more than 50 years of experience, offering curtains and interior drapery, sun awnings and pergolas, mattresses, beds and bed linen. The shop combines a wide choice of fabrics and products with advice on how to choose them, helping customers turn a window, a bedroom or a terrace into a comfortable, well-designed space.

My role in the project

I managed the brand’s social media presence on Instagram and Facebook, designing the graphics for the posts and giving the feed a coherent, elegant visual identity. I mainly developed educational and advice-led content, such as how colour changes with natural and artificial light, how to get more privacy without losing light, or how to check a sun awning before spring, mixing interior photography with a clear typographic hierarchy and the brand’s refined wordmark. Alongside the posts, I also worked on the brand’s web presence with WordPress and Elementor, keeping the tone of voice and the look consistent across social channels and website.
